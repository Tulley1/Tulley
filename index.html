📚 How Biotech Catalysts Typically Work

  • Phase 3 "topline" data is usually announced via press release weeks/months before full results are presented at a medical conference
  • FDA target action dates (PDUFA dates) are typically set ~6 months (priority review) or ~10 months (standard review) after an application is accepted
  • Data releases often cluster around major medical conferences: J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference (Jan), AACR (Apr), ASCO (Jun), ESMO (Sep), ASH (Dec)
  • Companies often guide expected readout timing ("mid-2026", "H2") during earnings calls or investor presentations — watch quarterly updates for changes to that guidance
  • This tab flags recent news containing trial/regulatory language — it does not predict exact dates
